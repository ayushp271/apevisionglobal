Six businesses. One vision.

Manpower Supply, Visa & Overseas Education

Ape Vision LLP

Manpower supply, visa & overseas education services

Connecting skilled talent with global opportunities.

Ape Vision LLP is a member of the Ape Vision Global, specialising in connecting skilled talent with global opportunities and supporting individuals and organisations through every stage of the manpower deployment and overseas education process - from sourcing and documentation to visa facilitation and successful placement.

We work closely with employers, institutions and individuals to deliver reliable, transparent and efficient services tailored to their specific needs, drawing on the strength, network and credibility of the wider Ape Vision Global.

Key Services
01
Manpower Supply

Sourcing and deploying skilled talent for employers and organisations, managing the process from selection through to successful placement.

02
Visa & Related Services

End-to-end visa facilitation and documentation support guiding individuals and organisations through every step of the process.

03
Overseas Education

Supporting students through admissions, documentation and visa processes for study abroad, in coordination with partner institutions.

Engineering, EPC & Infrastructure Development

Ape Vision Techinfra Pvt. Ltd.

Engineering, EPC & infrastructure development

Engineering the infrastructure of tomorrow.

Ape Vision Techinfra Pvt. Ltd. is the flagship engineering and infrastructure company of Ape Vision Global, focused on delivering sustainable, technology-driven and future-ready infrastructure solutions across India and international markets.

The company develops, engineers, procures, constructs and manages technologically advanced projects across data centres, green hydrogen, Napier grass to CBG and renewable energy, operating through both Engineering, Procurement & Construction (EPC) and Developer / Investor models - enabling it to participate across the project lifecycle from concept and engineering through construction, commissioning and long-term asset ownership.

Core Areas of Expertise
01
AI-Enabled Data Centres

Developing infrastructure for the rapidly expanding digital economy across hyperscale, enterprise and edge data centres.

02
Utility-Scale Solar PV Projects

Engineering and developing large-scale solar generation infrastructure supporting the transition toward cleaner energy.

03
Green Hydrogen & Napier Grass to CBG

Developing technologically advanced projects that convert renewable feedstocks into clean fuels and compressed biogas.

04
Energy Storage & Emerging Clean Technologies

Exploring energy storage and emerging clean technology solutions that can strengthen the future energy ecosystem.
