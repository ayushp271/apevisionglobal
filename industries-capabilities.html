Industries & Capabilities

One group. Six companies. Each with one job.

Ape Vision Global is a diversified, entrepreneur-led group. Rather than one company doing everything, each business below owns a specific part of the Group's work — so it's always clear who does what.

What the Group does

Four things, done end-to-end.

Across every business, Ape Vision Global works the same opportunity from four angles — advising on it, building it, trading around it, and investing in it.

Advise & Build

Engineering & EPC

Feasibility, design, procurement and construction for infrastructure and energy projects.

Trade & Source

Global Trade

Sourcing, distribution and cross-border commodity trading across sectors.

Invest & Develop

Investment & Real Estate

Capital deployed into diversified businesses, assets and property development.

Serve & Give Back

People, Retail & CSR

Manpower and visa services, consumer retail, and community-focused initiatives.
